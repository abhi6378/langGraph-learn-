AI: Good question. Here’s the difference, and how they relate in practice:

What “text generation model” means
- Core goal: produce fluent, coherent text given some input.
- Outputs: sentences, paragraphs, translations, summaries, style/motion of writing.
- How it’s judged: fluency, readability, usefulness of the produced text.
- Typical prompts: “Write a short email,” “summarize this article,” “translate to French,” etc.

What a “reasoning model” means
- Core goal: derive correct conclusions, solve problems, or plan steps.
- Outputs: solutions with reasoning steps, answers to math/logic questions, action plans, decision trees.
- How it’s judged: accuracy, logical consistency, the quality of the final answer, sometimes the usefulness of the steps.
- How it’s prompted: prompts that ask for steps, justifications, or explicit planning (e.g., “Let’s think step by step,” “show the calculations,” “plan a schedule”).

Key differences (in one place)
- Objective
  - Generation: maximize fluent, plausible text.
  - Reasoning: maximize correct conclusions or plans.
- Output style
  - Generation: text only (though it can include structured formats like bullet lists).
  - Reasoning: may include step-by-step calculations or a plan; sometimes the model is asked for a final answer plus a brief justification.
- Internal process
  - Both share the same underlying model. “Reasoning steps” can be prompted, but there isn’t a separate, standalone reasoning engine.
- Evaluation
  - Generation: quality of language, coherence, style.
  - Reasoning: correctness of the result, logical coherence, and sometimes the validity of the steps.
- Tools and data
  - Reasoning tasks often benefit from external tools (calculators, code execution, web search); generation tasks focus on language quality.

How to use prompts effectively
- To emphasize generation: specify style, tone, length, or audience.
- To emphasize reasoning: ask for step-by-step solutions, but note that I won’t reveal private internal thoughts. I can provide a concise answer plus a brief justification or essential calculations.
- If you want to check work: I can give a final answer with a short explanation and you can verify with your own steps or tools.
